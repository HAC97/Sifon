## Follow-up (2026-10-07)

Fixed: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6. Not touched (not approved): 7 (44px targets), 8 (reason for the disabled x only in `title`).
- 1: the `.drop.locked:focus-within` reset is gone, the bar keeps its ring.
- 2, 3: `focusLost()` in `web/app.js`; focus goes to `#title` (`tabindex="-1"`) when the video is found, to `#cancel` when a download starts, to `#again` or `#download` when it ends, to `#url` when a search fails.
- 4: new token `--edge` (light `#767b8a`, 3.64:1 on the page and 4.22:1 on the card; dark `#6b7287`, 3.97:1 and 3.63:1) on the bar, select, segment group, ghost buttons and the progress track.
- 5: the selected segment has an inset 2px accent ring.
- 6: `@media (forced-colors: active)` outlines for the bar focus and the selected and focused segment.
- Guards: `tests/test_web.py` (contrast of every token pair, both themes; `--edge` on the controls; forced-colors block; `tabindex`) and `tests/e2e/test_ui_smoke.py` (focus moves, ring on the fixed link, selection cue, forced-colors light and dark). All 14 new tests fail on the old `web/` and pass now; full downloader suite plus e2e: 531 passed.
- Checked by eye: screenshots of the running page in light and dark during a download.
